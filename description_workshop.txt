[h1]Exxo Avalon: Origins[/h1]

[h2]Information[/h2]

Avalon started out as a tConfig mod over a decade ago, and is now finally available for tModLoader!

Welcome to Exxo Avalon: Origins! Avalon is a vanilla-oriented mod, expanding on content while keeping to a generally vanilla feel. In this pre-Hardmode release, there are:
[list]
[*] Several new structures, including the Ice Shrine, the Lava Shrine, and the Evil Shrine.
[*] A new world evil, the Contagion, which is an alternate to the Corruption and Crimson.
[*] A stamina mechanic, which is completely optional, but allows you to do things such as teleporting and sprinting without the need for an accessory (this mechanic is currently unfinished but in a somewhat usable state).
[*] Several new prefixes (including some for armor only).
[*] Mystical Tomes, which are similar to accessories, but also grant stat boosts.
[*] Plenty more to explore!
[/list]

If you want to be informed about previous updates, check the change notes section for patch notes.

[h2]Links[/h2]

You can also have a look at our [url=https://https://terrariamods.wiki.gg/wiki/Avalon]official wiki[/url] for information.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Avalon is compatible with Achievements, Tokens, Biome Colored Lava, and Boss Checklist. There are more mods to be supported eventually.
